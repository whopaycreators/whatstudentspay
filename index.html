University College London🇬🇧 London, UK

Visa cost: £558 visa + £776/yr health surcharge

Money you must show: £1,529/mo (London, up to 9 mo); £1,570 from 30 Nov 2026

Part-time pay: £12.71/hr (21+, from 1 Apr 2026)

Rent: £650–1,300/mo shared private flat (plus bills); UCL halls avg £320/wk (about £1,390/mo)

Food: £229/mo groceries

UCL's own estimate. Source: UCL · checked 19 Oct 2026

New York University🇺🇸 New York, USA

Visa cost: $185 visa + $350 SEVIS fee

Money you must show: Set by your school's I-20 (no single gov't figure)

Part-time pay: Set by state – check state labor dept.

Rent: Housing + food together: $26,438/yr (about $2,940/mo)

Food: Included in the housing figure above

NYU's 2026-27 estimate. Yearly figures ÷ 9 months (Sept–May). Source: NYU · checked 19 Oct 2026
