Privacy

Last updated 15 Oct 2026.

When you use the "Add your costs" form, we collect what you type: your university, city, and the costs you share. Your email is optional and is only used to let you know when your school's page has real numbers added.

We don't sell your information or share it with anyone else. Form answers go straight to our inbox and are used to build the cost pages on this site.

We don't use tracking cookies or ads on this site.

Questions? Use the form on the main page.
