Privacy

Last updated 19 October 2026

The short version

When you add your costs, we collect what you type into the form. We use it to show real student costs on this site and to send you your unlocked school page. We don't sell your email address. You can ask us to delete your entry at any time.

What we collect

  • What you type in the form: your university, city, rent, food, part-time pay, surprise fees, visa cost, your email, and (if you add one) the email of the friend who invited you.
  • Basic technical data that any website receives, such as your IP address and browser type, kept by our hosting and form providers.

Please only add a friend's email if they invited you and are happy for you to share it.

Why we use it

  • Cost numbers are combined with other students' reports and shown on the site without your name or email.
  • Your email is used to send you what you signed up for (your unlocked school page, and news about Pro if you asked for it), and to credit referral rewards.

We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who else handles it

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers form entries to us by email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.

These services may process data outside your country. We don't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep entries while the site is running and they're useful for showing costs. We delete your email and entry when you ask.

Age

This site is for students and parents planning university study. You must be 16 or older to submit the form. If you're younger, please ask a parent or guardian to submit it for you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the UK, the EU, Australia or California), you can ask to see, correct or delete the information you gave us, or to stop us using it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Contact us

To ask about your data or have it deleted, send us a message below using the email you signed up with. We'll reply by email.

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
